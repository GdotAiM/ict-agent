# ICT Bread and Butter Buy-Setup — 4-Session Intraday Bullish Model with Offset & Re-Accumulation

**Source:** [https://innercircletrader.net/tutorials/ict-bread-and-butter-buy-setup/](https://innercircletrader.net/tutorials/ict-bread-and-butter-buy-setup/)

ICT Bread and Butter Buy-Setup is an intraday trading model designed to capture at least 30 to 50 pips per session. Built on the two IPDA price engines — Offset-Accumulation and Re-Accumulation — it produces 2 to 3 buy setups per day across the London, New York, London Close and Asia sessions.

This guide focuses specifically on the BUY side of the ICT Bread and Butter model. For the sell-side variant see my [ICT Bread and Butter](<https://innercircletrader.net/tutorials/ict-bread-and-butter/>) overview guide.

In this guide I walk you through the buy-setup — the two engines (offset-accumulation, re-accumulation), the four session entries (London, NY, London Close, Asia), the realistic objectives, the risk-reward profile, the step-by-step trade flow, common mistakes and the FAQ.

#### What is ICT Bread and Butter Buy-Setup?

ICT Bread and Butter Buy-Setup is an intraday model that focuses on bullish days.

When the market is poised to go higher based on higher-timeframe institutional order flow, we expect the opening at or near the low of the daily range.

There can be a small decline below the opening price.

London open posts the initial leg higher intraday, then waits for the New York open.

New York open sees a continuation of higher prices to 10:00 AM New York time.

Typically between 10:00 AM to 12:00 PM New York time, expect the high of the day to form — and then normally price will retrace lower.

In this buy program, [IPDA](<https://innercircletrader.net/tutorials/ict-ipda/>) will perform one of two price-engine models.

**(I) Offset-Accumulation:** IPDA will reprice the market below an old low to promote the sell stops residing there for current long holders — and this action engineers the sellers at a deep discount.

This happens frequently in a bullish market when the higher-timeframe institutional order flow suggests higher prices.

You can see the picture below showing the idea of offset-accumulation.

![ICT Bread and Butter offset-accumulation example — IPDA reprices the market below an old low to engineer sell stops, then reverses with a strong bullish move for long entries](https://innercircletrader.net/wp-content/uploads/2025/01/ICT-Bread-and-butter-Offsetaccumulation.png)

**(II) Re-Accumulation:** IPDA will reprice the market lower to a fair-value price array to provide smart money a discount price for long entries.

The retracement lower in price places pain on current long holders and tends to induce selling — thus providing the sell-side liquidity to pair the smart money long entries with.

You can see the picture below showing the idea of re-accumulation.

![ICT Bread and Butter re-accumulation example — IPDA reprices the market lower to a fair value PD array, inducing sells before reversing higher for the buy entry](https://innercircletrader.net/wp-content/uploads/2025/01/ICT-Bread-and-butter-Reaccumulation.png)

#### Realistic Objectives

Trade duration: typically 1 to 2 hours or less.

Pips per trade: 15 to 30 pips, or maximum 50 pips.

Timing chart interval: 5-minute.

#### Frequency of Setups

Per week: as many as 15 or more.

Per day: 2 to 3 setups typically.

Per session: 1 per session typically — London, New York, London Close and Asia.

#### Risk-to-Reward Ratio

Risk-reward ratio typically 1:1, but it can be better.

Ideal risk per trade is 0.5% to 1%.

#### ICT Bread and Butter — London Open Setup

When the higher-timeframe institutional order flow is bullish, anticipate the London session low of the day formation.

The open at 0 GMT or 12:00 AM New York time can see a protraction phase lower in price — this can be scalped from the open or just above it prior to 10:00 AM New York time.

The classic London [Judas Swing](<https://innercircletrader.net/tutorials/ict-judas-swing-complete-guide/>) lower can be scalped easier.

The market retraces 05:00 AM to 07:00 AM New York time, and this can provide a long scalp entry — once a discount [PD Array](<https://innercircletrader.net/tutorials/ict-pd-array-key-to-trade-execution/>) is hit.

#### ICT Bread and Butter — The New York Setup

When London open confirms institutional sponsorship on the long side and posts the daily low, expect to see the New York open continue higher — unless a higher-timeframe premium array has been hit intraday.

Look for the intraday swings higher to determine the [discount](<https://innercircletrader.net/tutorials/ict-premium-and-discount-zone-identification/>) range to go long at, in the [NY open killzone](<https://innercircletrader.net/tutorials/master-ict-kill-zones/>).

Use 08:20 AM New York time (CME Chicago Mercantile Exchange) to anticipate the New York Judas Swing to fade.

Target is the next higher-timeframe premium array found on the 1-hour, 4-hour or daily chart.

#### ICT Bread and Butter — London Close Setup

When the New York and London sessions have moved in tandem and the higher-timeframe PD array has been reached — and it is at least 10:30 AM New York time — expect a retracement off the day’s high between 10:30 AM and 01:00 PM.

Look for the 5-minute chart failure swing at the high and a bearish order block to execute the trade.

Risk only 10 pips above the day’s high and target 20 to 30% of the total daily range on the retracement lower.

Keep in mind this trade can be very difficult to see often, and the daily range can exceed the higher-timeframe PD array.

#### ICT Bread and Butter — Asia Open Setup

When the higher-timeframe institutional order flow is bullish, enter long at or just under the 0 GMT open price (07:00 PM New York time) and expect an expansion of 15 to 20 pips higher as the Asian range is established.

Always aim for the 15 to 20 pips in this trade, as the range can be limited because it is the Asian range formation.

#### Step-by-Step Bread and Butter Buy Flow

This is the exact sequence I run for any Bread and Butter buy setup, regardless of which session.

  1. **Confirm higher-timeframe bullish institutional order flow.** 1-day and 4-hour structure plus the higher-timeframe PD array.
  2. **Set your chart to New York local time.** Every session window in the model uses NY time.
  3. **Identify the active session.** London (00:00 NY) / NY (07:00 NY) / London Close (10:30 NY) / Asia (19:00 NY).
  4. **Mark the discount PD array for that session.** Discount FVG, order block or breaker below the opening price.
  5. **Wait for the IPDA price engine.** Offset-accumulation (sweep below an old low) or re-accumulation (retrace to the PD array).
  6. **Drop to the 5-minute for the trigger.** Wait for the lower-timeframe MSS at the discount level.
  7. **Enter on the post-MSS retest.** At the post-MSS PD array, in the bullish direction.
  8. **Set the stop.** Beyond the swept discount extreme, with a small buffer.
  9. **Take 15 to 30 pips on the position.** Maximum 50 pips. Trail the runner only if the higher-timeframe premium array is still distant.
  10. **Risk 0.5% to 1% per trade.** Multiple setups per day require strict per-trade risk.

#### Best Pairs for ICT Bread and Butter Buy

The model works on every major instrument that respects the four session windows — GBP/USD, EUR/USD, USD/CAD, USD/JPY plus metals.

**For traders in the United States** who follow the CFTC FIFO and no-hedge rules, the Bread and Butter framework maps cleanly onto NQ and ES futures (CME Group) plus regulated forex pairs through US-based brokers. The 09:50 NY-AM [macro window](<https://innercircletrader.net/tutorials/ict-macro-time-based-strategy/>) is a particularly strong place to watch for the New York setup because the algorithmic delivery is most concentrated there.

#### Common Mistakes Around the Bread and Butter Buy

These are the recurring mistakes I see when traders first start trading the Bread and Butter buy.

  1. **Wrong timezone.** Every session window is defined in New York local time. Using your broker’s server time or local time produces the wrong windows.
  2. **Trading without confirmed bullish bias.** The buy-setup requires higher-timeframe bullish order flow. Without it, every discount looks like a setup.
  3. **Skipping the 5-minute MSS.** The discount PD array is the zone — the entry trigger is the 5-minute MSS. Touching the discount alone is not an entry.
  4. **Ignoring the session boundaries.** A ‘London’ trade taken at 06:00 AM NY is past the window. Stick to the session start times.
  5. **Greedy targets.** 15 to 30 pips is the model target. Holding for 100 pips converts the scalp into a swing and breaks the model.
  6. **Trading the London Close every day.** The London Close setup is the hardest — only take it when both London and NY have moved in tandem AND a higher-timeframe PD array has been reached.

#### FAQs about ICT Bread and Butter Buy-Setup

Brief answers to the questions readers ask most often about the Bread and Butter buy-setup.

**What is the ICT Bread and Butter Buy-Setup?**

An intraday bullish model that captures 30 to 50 pips per session, powered by two IPDA price engines — offset-accumulation and re-accumulation — across the London, NY, London Close and Asia sessions.

**What is offset-accumulation?**

IPDA reprices the market below an old low to promote the sell stops residing there for current long holders, engineering sellers at a deep discount before reversing higher.

**What is re-accumulation?**

IPDA reprices the market lower to a fair-value PD array to provide smart money discount prices for long entries — inducing selling that pairs with the smart-money long entry.

**How many setups per day?**

2 to 3 setups typically — one per session across London, New York, London Close and Asia.

**What is the realistic pip target?**

15 to 30 pips per trade, with a maximum of 50 pips. Trade duration typically 1 to 2 hours.

**What is the risk-reward ratio?**

Typically 1:1 — but can be better. Risk 0.5% to 1% per trade.

**What chart interval do I use?**

5-minute for the execution. 1-hour or 4-hour for the higher-timeframe PD array.

**When does the London Open setup trigger?**

The market retraces between 05:00 AM and 07:00 AM NY time after the London Judas Swing — that retracement into a discount PD array is the long scalp entry.

**When does the New York Open setup trigger?**

08:20 AM New York time (CME open) — anticipate the New York Judas Swing to fade, then enter long at the intraday discount.

**When does the London Close setup trigger?**

Between 10:30 AM and 01:00 PM NY time — but only after London and NY have moved in tandem AND a higher-timeframe PD array has been reached. This trade can be hard to see often.

**When does the Asia Open setup trigger?**

At or just under the 0 GMT open (07:00 PM NY time) — expect a 15 to 20 pip expansion higher as the Asian range forms.

**Does the Bread and Butter buy-setup work on indices and gold?**

Yes — NQ, ES and XAU/USD all produce textbook Bread and Butter buy setups, especially during the New York AM session and around US economic releases.

**What is the difference between the buy-setup and the sell-setup?**

The buy-setup focuses on bullish institutional order flow days. The sell-setup is the mirror image — see my [ICT Bread and Butter](<https://innercircletrader.net/tutorials/ict-bread-and-butter/>) guide for the broader overview.
