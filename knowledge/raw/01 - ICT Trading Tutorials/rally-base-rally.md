# Rally Base Rally (RBR) — Bullish Continuation Pattern with 70:30 / 25:75 Body-Wick Rules

**Source:** [https://innercircletrader.net/tutorials/rally-base-rally/](https://innercircletrader.net/tutorials/rally-base-rally/)

![Rally Base Rally — supply and demand bullish continuation pattern showing initial rally, consolidation base, and second rally after breakout](https://www.innercircletrader.net/wp-content/uploads/2023/12/Rally-base-rally-strategy.png)

Rally Base Rally (RBR) is a powerful bullish continuation pattern from the supply and demand framework — an initial rally, a consolidation base, and a second rally after the base breaks. It is one of the cleanest continuation setups inside an established bullish trend, and once you can identify the body-to-wick ratio rules, the pattern becomes very visible on the chart.

Rally Base Rally is one of the various strategies used in technical analysis to predict the future move of any asset — forex currency pairs, commodities, crypto, stocks or indices. This strategy originates from smart money concepts, and its foundation lies in [Supply and Demand](<https://www.innercircletrader.net/tutorials/supply-and-demand-in-forex-trading/>).

In this guide I walk you through Rally Base Rally — the definition, the three-component identification, the candle body-to-wick rules, the trade execution, the confirmation filters, the step-by-step trade flow, common mistakes and the FAQ.

#### What is Rally Base Rally?

Rally Base Rally is a powerful bullish continuation concept from the supply and demand trading strategy. The RBR concept states that prices move in a series of rallies and consolidations — these rallies and consolidations create a pattern that can be identified and traded easily.

![Rally Base Rally illustration — initial bullish rally, sideways base consolidation, then a second bullish rally extending the trend higher](https://www.innercircletrader.net/wp-content/uploads/2023/12/Rally-base-rally.png)

The rally phase occurs when prices move sharply on the buy side, indicating strong demand. The base phase is marked by consolidation of prices in a range as the market digests the previous sharp move. The 2nd rally phase starts after the breakout of the previous consolidation range and prices again move sharply to the buy side.

#### How to Identify Rally Base Rally

To trade a Rally Base Rally pattern, it is essential to identify and mark it correctly on the price chart.

Rally Base Rally pattern consists of 3 main components:

**(I)** Initial Rally

**(II)** The Base

**(III)** Subsequent Rally

The initial rally is a sharp move toward the buy side, followed by a period of consolidation in the base phase, and finally a second rally after the breakout of the base — showing continuation of the previous bullish move.

![Rally Base Rally three-component breakdown — sharp initial rally, narrow horizontal base, breakout above the base followed by the second rally continuation](https://www.innercircletrader.net/wp-content/uploads/2023/12/rally-base-rally-2.png)

#### Body-to-Wick Ratio of Candles for Rally Base Rally

To confirm an initial rally, look for two or more consecutive bullish candles with a body-to-wick ratio of 70:30 or more — meaning the body should cover at least 70% of the total candlestick and the wick should not be more than 30% of the total candlestick.

The base is characterized by a single or more candlesticks that show a temporary pause in the bullish momentum. The body-to-wick ratio of base candles should be 25:75 or less — meaning the body should cover 25% or less of the total candlestick while the wick should cover 75% or more.

This body-to-wick filter is what separates a real RBR from a noisy retracement — the rally candles are decisive, the base candles are indecisive, and the directional change is structurally visible.

#### How to Trade Rally Base Rally

To trade a Rally Base Rally pattern, you should wait for the formation of the initial rally and the base. After the formation and identification of the initial rally and the base, you should wait for price to break the base.

A candle closing above the price range of the base area is considered the breakout of the base.

After the breakout of the base, you can take a buy trade immediately if price retests the base area. If it does not retest the base area immediately, you can wait for price to retest the base area and execute a buy trade at this retest.

![Rally Base Rally trade execution example — base breakout candle above the consolidation range, retest of the base, then buy entry with stop below the base low](https://www.innercircletrader.net/wp-content/uploads/2023/12/RBR-trade-1.png)

While trading a Rally Base Rally pattern, your stop loss will be below the low of the base, and for take profit you can target a 1:2 risk-reward or the next resistance zone.

#### Other Confirmations to Execute a Trade

To execute a buy trade after breakout and retest of the base, look for extra confirmations.

**Bullish Candlestick Pattern Confirmation** can be very useful while trading Rally Base Rally. Any bullish candlestick pattern like a hammer, morning star or engulfing will increase the probability of winning.

**Volume Confirmation** is also essential while trading Rally Base Rally. A strong breakout from the base phase accompanied by high volume signals a high-probability trade entry.

**Lower-Timeframe MSS Confirmation** — ICT traders can drop to the 5-minute or 15-minute and wait for a [Market Structure Shift](<https://innercircletrader.net/tutorials/ict-market-structure-shift/>) at the retest of the base for the tightest entry trigger.

#### Rally Base Rally in the ICT Framework

RBR maps cleanly onto ICT concepts. The initial rally is the displacement leg that produced a [break of structure](<https://innercircletrader.net/tutorials/break-of-structure-bos/>). The base is the demand zone — specifically the consolidation that smart money uses for accumulation. The base low is often the [inducement](<https://innercircletrader.net/tutorials/what-is-inducement-in-forex/>) level, and the breakout-and-retest entry pairs naturally with the post-inducement-sweep MSS trigger.

If you trade ICT, treat RBR as a visual shortcut for the bullish BOS-plus-demand setup, and use the body-to-wick ratio as a quick filter for whether the leg is high-conviction.

#### Step-by-Step Rally Base Rally Trade Flow

This is the exact sequence I run when trading Rally Base Rally.

  1. **Confirm bullish trend.** Higher-timeframe structure must be bullish.
  2. **Identify the initial rally.** Two or more consecutive bullish candles with body-to-wick ratio of 70:30 or more.
  3. **Mark the base.** One or more consolidation candles with body-to-wick ratio of 25:75 or less.
  4. **Wait for the breakout.** A candle closes above the high of the base.
  5. **Wait for the retest of the base.** Price retraces back to the base area.
  6. **Wait for confirmation.** Bullish candlestick pattern, volume spike, or lower-timeframe MSS at the base retest.
  7. **Enter the buy.** On the confirmation candle close.
  8. **Set the stop.** Below the low of the base, with a small buffer.
  9. **Set the target.** 1:2 risk-reward, or the next resistance / liquidity zone.
  10. **Manage the trade.** Book partials at 1:1, trail to the full target.

#### Best Pairs for Rally Base Rally

Rally Base Rally works on every major instrument that produces trending moves — GBP/USD, EUR/USD, USD/CAD, plus metals such as XAU/USD and XAG/USD, and indices like NQ and ES.

**For traders in the United States** who follow the CFTC FIFO and no-hedge rules, the RBR framework maps cleanly onto NQ and ES futures (CME Group) plus regulated forex pairs through US-based brokers. The 09:50 NY-AM [macro window](<https://innercircletrader.net/tutorials/ict-macro-time-based-strategy/>) is a particularly strong place to watch for RBR breakouts.

#### Common Mistakes Around Rally Base Rally

These are the recurring mistakes I see when traders first start trading Rally Base Rally.

  1. **Ignoring the body-to-wick ratio.** Without the 70:30 rally and 25:75 base filter, every pullback looks like an RBR. The ratio is the structural filter.
  2. **Trading RBR against the trend.** The pattern is a bullish CONTINUATION setup. In a bearish trend, the inverse pattern (Drop Base Drop) applies — not RBR.
  3. **Entering at the breakout without retest.** Many breakouts are fakeouts. Wait for the retest of the base, plus a confirmation candle.
  4. **Marking the base too small.** A base needs at least 2 to 3 indecisive candles. A single candle pause is too thin to qualify.
  5. **Tight stops at the base low.** Stops parked exactly at the base low get hunted. Place the stop with a buffer below the base low.
  6. **No risk-reward discipline.** The model targets 1:2 minimum. Holding for less than 1:1 or chasing 1:5+ both break the model statistics.

#### FAQs about Rally Base Rally

Brief answers to the questions readers ask most often about Rally Base Rally.

**What is Rally Base Rally?**

A bullish continuation pattern from the supply and demand framework consisting of three components — an initial rally, a consolidation base, and a second rally after the base breaks.

**How do I identify Rally Base Rally?**

Find a bullish trend, identify the initial rally (2+ candles with 70:30 body-to-wick ratio), mark the consolidation base (candles with 25:75 body-to-wick ratio), and wait for the breakout above the base.

**What is the body-to-wick rule?**

Rally candles need body ≥ 70% of total candle height (wick ≤ 30%). Base candles need body ≤ 25% of total candle height (wick ≥ 75%). This filter separates real rallies from noisy moves.

**What is the difference between Rally Base Rally and Drop Base Drop?**

RBR is the bullish version (rally → base → rally). DBD is the bearish mirror (drop → base → drop). Same structural rules, opposite direction.

**Where do I enter the trade?**

After the base breakout, either enter immediately on the retest of the base, or wait for price to retrace back to the base and enter at the retest with a bullish confirmation candle.

**Where do I place the stop loss?**

Below the low of the base, with a small buffer.

**Where do I take profit?**

1:2 risk-reward minimum, or the next resistance / liquidity zone. Some traders trail with the trend for larger targets.

**What timeframe works best for RBR?**

RBR appears on every timeframe. For day trading use 15-minute and 1-hour. For swing trading use 4-hour and daily.

**How does RBR fit in the ICT framework?**

The initial rally is the BOS displacement leg. The base is the demand zone (often containing the order block). The breakout-and-retest is the post-inducement continuation play.

**What confirmations do I add?**

Bullish candlestick patterns (hammer, morning star, engulfing), high-volume breakout, or a lower-timeframe MSS at the base retest.

**Does Rally Base Rally work on indices and gold?**

Yes — NQ, ES and XAU/USD all produce textbook RBR setups, especially during the New York AM session.

**What is the success rate of RBR?**

In a confirmed bullish trend with a clean rally and tight base meeting the body-to-wick rules, RBR historically delivers a 60–75% breakout-success rate when the retest is waited out.
