# Break of Structure (BOS) in Forex — Bullish/Bearish, Major vs Minor & BOS vs MSS vs CHOCH

**Source:** [https://innercircletrader.net/tutorials/break-of-structure-bos/](https://innercircletrader.net/tutorials/break-of-structure-bos/)

![Break of Structure \(BOS\) in trading — smart money concept showing bullish BOS as a higher high above the previous structure after the inducement is grabbed](https://www.innercircletrader.net/wp-content/uploads/2024/01/break-of-structure.png)

Break of Structure (BOS) is the smart-money concept that confirms a trend is continuing — price breaks its previous structure in the direction of the existing trend, after the inducement has been taken. BOS is the level that lets you mark the order flow, the order block and the next valid pullback inside the leg.

In this guide I walk you through the break of structure concept — the meaning, the bullish and bearish variants, the major-vs-minor distinction, the BOS vs MSS comparison, the step-by-step trade flow, common mistakes and the FAQ.

#### What is Break of Structure in Trading?

Break of structure is the continuation of the market trend by breaking its previous structure.

Break of structure helps us catch continuation trades and identify the market trend.

A bullish break of structure indicates higher prices in the future, while a bearish break of structure indicates lower prices in the future.

You can see the example of BOS in the picture given below.

![Break of structure example — clean break above the previous swing high after price grabbed the inducement, confirming continuation of the bullish trend](https://innercircletrader.net/wp-content/uploads/2024/01/Break-of-structure-BOS.png)

#### How to Identify Break of Structure

Before identifying break of structure you should know about [Inducement](<https://www.innercircletrader.net/tutorials/what-is-inducement-in-forex/>), because the break of previous structure after taking the inducement is the valid break of structure.

On the basis of market trend, break of structure can be divided into two types — bullish and bearish.

#### (I) Bullish Break of Structure

When price is in a bullish trend it makes higher highs — meaning it always breaks its previous high making a new high — and this is called a bullish break of structure.

But break of structure is incomplete without inducement, so a break of structure in a bullish trend happens when price grabs the inducement and breaks the previous high making a higher high.

You can see the real market example of bullish break of structure in the picture given below.

![Bullish break of structure real market example — price grabs the inducement low, then breaks the previous swing high to confirm a higher high BOS](https://www.innercircletrader.net/wp-content/uploads/2024/01/Bullish-break-of-structure.png)

If price breaks the previous high without grabbing the inducement, it is called a minor break of structure — a new high is found but the previous low remains intact.

You can see the example of bullish minor BOS in the picture given below.

![Bullish minor BOS example — price breaks the previous swing high without first grabbing the inducement, shifting only the high while the low remains intact](https://innercircletrader.net/wp-content/uploads/2024/01/Bullish-Minor-Bos.png)

#### (II) Bearish Break of Structure

When price is in a bearish trend it makes lower lows — meaning it always breaks its previous low making a new low — and this is known as a bearish break of structure.

But break of structure is incomplete without inducement, so a break of structure in a bearish trend happens when price grabs the inducement and breaks the previous low making a lower low.

You can see the example of bearish break of structure in the picture given below.

![Bearish break of structure real market example — price grabs the inducement high, then breaks the previous swing low to confirm a lower low BOS](https://www.innercircletrader.net/wp-content/uploads/2024/01/bearish-break-of-structure.png)

If price breaks the previous low without taking out the inducement it is called a minor break of structure — a new lower low is formed but the previous lower high remains the same.

You can see the example of bearish minor BOS in the picture given below.

![Bearish minor BOS example — price breaks the previous swing low without first grabbing the inducement, shifting only the low while the high remains intact](https://innercircletrader.net/wp-content/uploads/2024/01/Bearish-Minor-BOS.png)

#### Major vs Minor Break of Structure

The single rule that separates a major BOS from a minor BOS is whether the inducement was taken before the break.

Major BOS — price grabbed the inducement first, then broke the structure. This is the high-conviction continuation signal that confirms the structural shift.

Minor BOS — price broke the structure WITHOUT first taking the inducement. The high (or low) shifts, but the rest of the market structure remains intact, and a new inducement has to be marked. See my [Inducement After Break of Structure](<https://innercircletrader.net/tutorials/inducement-after-break-of-structure/>) guide for the full breakdown.

#### Best Timeframe to Identify Break of Structure

Market structure appears across all timeframes, and different timeframes often display different market structures.

So you can identify break of structure on any timeframe by marking the previous structure.

The best approach is to have a clear understanding of the market as a whole — this includes low timeframe, medium timeframe and high timeframe structure. Think about each timeframe and align the market structure as a top-down analysis.

#### How to Trade Break of Structure in Forex

**In a bullish market** , when price breaks the structure you should mark the inducement and wait for price to retrace back and take the inducement.

When price grabs the inducement you can look for buy trades with confirmations like a [Market Structure Shift](<https://innercircletrader.net/tutorials/ict-market-structure-shift/>) or trend reversal on the lower timeframe.

**In a bearish market** , when the market breaks the structure you should mark the inducement and wait for price to retrace back and take the inducement.

When price grabs the inducement you can look for sell trades with confirmations like a market structure shift or trend reversal on the lower timeframe.

#### Step-by-Step BOS Trade Flow

This is the exact sequence I run when trading a break of structure.

  1. **Read the higher-timeframe context.** 1-day and 4-hour charts — bullish, bearish or neutral structure.
  2. **Identify the previous structure.** The most recent swing high (bullish) or swing low (bearish) on the analysis timeframe.
  3. **Wait for the BOS to print.** A clean break above the previous swing high (bullish) or below the previous swing low (bearish).
  4. **Mark the inducement inside the leg.** The first valid pullback inside the leg that produced the BOS — this is what defines a major BOS once swept.
  5. **Wait for price to retrace and grab the inducement.** Bullish — price dips below the inducement low. Bearish — price spikes above the inducement high.
  6. **Wait for confirmation.** A lower-timeframe MSS, fair value gap or order block formed after the inducement sweep.
  7. **Enter on the retest.** Take the trade in the higher-timeframe direction at the post-sweep PD array.
  8. **Set the stop.** Beyond the swept inducement extreme, with a small buffer.
  9. **Take profit at the next draw on liquidity.** Old high or low, relative equal level, or higher-timeframe FVG.

#### ICT MSS vs BOS

Break of structure indicates the break of previous structure (high or low) AFTER taking out the inducement.

ICT market structure shift indicates the break of a [Swing Low](<https://innercircletrader.net/tutorials/ict-swing-low-formation/>) or [Swing High](<https://innercircletrader.net/tutorials/ict-swing-high-explained/>).

So the difference between break of structure and market structure shift is just inducement.

Break of structure indicates the larger shift in the price trend, while market structure shift indicates the initial shift in the structure.

You can see the picture below explaining BOS vs MSS.

![ICT MSS vs BOS comparison — MSS breaks a swing high or low to signal initial reversal, while BOS breaks the previous structure after the inducement is taken to confirm continuation](https://innercircletrader.net/wp-content/uploads/2024/01/MSS-vs-BOS-1.png)

#### BOS vs CHOCH

Break of structure (BOS) confirms the continuation of the existing trend. [Change of character (CHOCH)](<https://innercircletrader.net/tutorials/change-of-character-choch-in-trading/>) confirms the reversal of the existing trend.

In a bullish trend, BOS is the break above the previous swing high. CHOCH is the break below the most recent higher low.

In a bearish trend, BOS is the break below the previous swing low. CHOCH is the break above the most recent lower high.

BOS = continuation. CHOCH = reversal.

Break of structure works on every major instrument — GBP/USD, EUR/USD, USD/CAD, plus metals such as XAU/USD and XAG/USD. The cleanest BOS prints come from instruments that produce strong displacement legs.

**For traders in the United States** who follow the CFTC FIFO and no-hedge rules, the BOS framework maps cleanly onto NQ and ES futures (CME Group) plus regulated forex pairs through US-based brokers. The 09:50 NY-AM [macro window](<https://innercircletrader.net/tutorials/ict-macro-time-based-strategy/>) is a particularly strong place to watch for BOS trades because the algorithmic delivery is most concentrated there.

#### Common Mistakes Around Break of Structure

These are the recurring mistakes I see when traders first start trading the break of structure concept.

  1. **Treating every wick as a BOS.** A BOS requires a clean close beyond the previous swing high or low — not just a wick. Wicks above the high without a close are stop hunts, not BOS prints.
  2. **Ignoring the inducement.** A BOS without an inducement sweep is a minor BOS at best. The full directional confirmation requires inducement to be taken.
  3. **Confusing BOS with CHOCH.** BOS is continuation. CHOCH is reversal. Mixing them up flips the trade direction entirely.
  4. **Trading the BOS without higher-timeframe alignment.** A BOS on the 15-minute against the daily bias has a much lower hit rate than a BOS aligned with the daily.
  5. **Entering at the BOS level itself.** The BOS is the structural confirmation. The entry is the post-inducement-sweep retest at a PD array — not the BOS candle.
  6. **Chasing the BOS after the move has already extended.** If price has already moved several ATRs past the BOS without retracing, the trade is gone. Wait for the next setup rather than chasing.

#### FAQs about Break of Structure

Brief answers to the questions readers ask most often about BOS.

**What is break of structure in trading?**

Break of structure is the smart-money concept that confirms a trend is continuing — price breaks its previous swing high (bullish) or swing low (bearish) after taking out the inducement. BOS is what lets you mark the order flow and the order block inside the leg.

**What is the difference between BOS and MSS?**

BOS = break of previous structure AFTER taking inducement (continuation). MSS = break of a swing high or swing low (initial reversal signal). The single difference is whether the inducement was taken first.

**What is the difference between BOS and CHOCH?**

BOS confirms the continuation of the existing trend. CHOCH confirms the reversal. In a bullish trend, BOS is a higher high; CHOCH is the break of the most recent higher low.

**What is a minor break of structure?**

Minor BOS is when price breaks the previous structure WITHOUT first grabbing the inducement. The high (or low) shifts, but the rest of the structure remains intact, and a new inducement has to be marked.

**How do I trade a break of structure?**

Wait for the BOS to print, mark the inducement inside the leg, wait for price to retrace and grab the inducement, then enter on the lower-timeframe MSS or order block confirmation. Stop beyond the swept inducement extreme; target the next draw on liquidity.

**What timeframe is best for spotting BOS?**

BOS works on every timeframe. For day trading, mark the BOS on the 15-minute or 1-hour and use the 1-day and 4-hour for the directional context.

**Where do I place the stop loss on a BOS trade?**

Beyond the swept inducement extreme — below the inducement low for buy trades, above the inducement high for sell trades — with a small buffer.

**Where do I take profit?**

The next draw on liquidity — old high or low, relative equal level, or higher-timeframe FVG.

**Does BOS work on indices and gold?**

Yes — NQ, ES and XAU/USD all produce textbook BOS prints, especially during the New York AM session and around US economic releases.

**What if price breaks the structure with a wick but no close?**

A wick without a close beyond the previous swing is not a valid BOS — that is a stop hunt. Wait for the candle to close beyond the structure for the BOS to be confirmed.

**Is the inducement required for every BOS?**

For a major BOS — yes. For a minor BOS — no. The minor BOS is the structural shift without the inducement; a major BOS requires the inducement to be swept first.
