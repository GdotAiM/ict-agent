# ICT Liquidity Void — What It Is, How to Identify & Trade It + Free PDF

A liquidity void is the imbalance left behind when price breaks a consolidation phase and moves strongly in one direction without retracement or wick overlap. It signals an absence of willing buyers or sellers in that price zone — and the algorithm typically returns to fill or partially reprice the void on a later leg.

#### What is an ICT Liquidity Void?

The word “liquidity” means willing buyers or sellers in the market. The word “void” means absence of something.

So an ICT liquidity void means the absence of buyers or sellers in the market — depending on the case.

When price breaks a consolidation phase and moves in one direction strongly without a retracement or wick inside the prior candlestick range, the imbalance created by those candlesticks is known as an ICT liquidity void.

Unlike a single 3-candle [fair value gap](<https://innercircletrader.net/tutorials/fair-value-gap-trading-strategy/>), a liquidity void can span multiple consecutive candles and may contain two or more fair value gaps inside it.

#### Liquidity Void Meaning (Quick Definition)

In ICT terminology, a **liquidity void** is a multi-candle imbalance zone created by a strong displacement move out of a consolidation. The void exists because there were no opposing orders to slow the move, so price covered the distance with no internal retracement. Price often returns later to reprice the void — partially or fully — before continuing in the original direction or reversing.

#### Bullish ICT Liquidity Void

A bullish liquidity void is created when price breaks the consolidation range and moves strongly to the buy side, targeting sell-side liquidity.

A bullish liquidity void is created by a [displacement move](<https://innercircletrader.net/tutorials/ict-displacement-move/> "Displacement Move"). Because of the absence of sell-side liquidity, price moves heavily to the buy side and creates the imbalance termed a bullish liquidity void.

![Bullish liquidity void diagram — strong displacement candles to the upside leaving multiple non-overlapping wick zones below](https://innercircletrader.net/wp-content/uploads/2024/04/bullish-liquidity-void.png)

**To trade a bullish liquidity void** you have to look at the market structure to determine if it is bullish or bearish.

In a bullish market, price retraces back and fills the liquidity void, after which it continues going higher. So you can look for buy setups inside a bullish liquidity void.

A bullish liquidity void acts as a strong support in a bullish market, as you can see in the picture below.

![Bullish liquidity void trade example — price retraces into the void and respects it as support, then continues higher](https://innercircletrader.net/wp-content/uploads/2024/04/bullish-liquidity-void-trading.png)

In a bearish market, bullish liquidity voids are not respected by price, and price will mostly continue going lower after repricing the liquidity void.

#### Bearish ICT Liquidity Void

When price breaks a consolidation range to the downside with strong momentum and large candles, targeting buy-side liquidity, it results in the formation of a bearish liquidity void.

It is formed because of the absence of buy-side liquidity — and price continues going lower without any retracement, making an imbalance known as a bearish liquidity void.

![Bearish liquidity void diagram — strong displacement candles to the downside leaving multiple non-overlapping wicks above](https://innercircletrader.net/wp-content/uploads/2024/04/bearish-liquidity-void.png)

**To trade a bearish liquidity void** first identify the market structure.

If the market structure is bearish, price will retrace back and fill the liquidity void, after which it will continue going lower. You can look for sell setups inside a bearish liquidity void.

Bearish liquidity voids act as a strong resistance in a bearish market, as you can see in the picture below.

![Bearish liquidity void trade example — price retraces into the void and respects it as resistance, then continues lower](https://innercircletrader.net/wp-content/uploads/2024/04/bearish-liquidity-void-trade.png)

If the market structure is bullish and a bearish liquidity void is created in a retracement move, then price may not respect the void and may continue higher even after repricing the bearish liquidity void.

#### Step-by-Step Liquidity Void Trade Flow

This is the exact sequence I run when trading a liquidity void.

  1. **Identify the higher-timeframe market structure.** Daily and H4 — bullish or bearish.
  2. **Spot the displacement move.** A strong directional break from a consolidation, large candles, no internal retracement.
  3. **Mark the liquidity void.** The price range covered by the displacement candles where wicks do not overlap.
  4. **Mark the consequent encroachment.** The 50% midpoint of the void — this is the most reactive level inside the imbalance.
  5. **Confirm bias alignment.** A bullish liquidity void in a bullish trend (or bearish in bearish trend) is high-probability. Counter-trend voids are not respected.
  6. **Wait for the retracement.** Price must trade back into the void.
  7. **Drop to the lower timeframe.** 5-minute or 15-minute for the entry trigger.
  8. **Wait for the LTF MSS.** A clean Market Structure Shift inside the void confirms the reaction.
  9. **Enter at the CE retest.** The 50% midpoint is the cleanest entry; a partial fill above or below is acceptable.
  10. **Set the stop.** Beyond the opposite edge of the void, or beyond the displacement candle’s high/low for tighter stops.
  11. **Take profit at the next draw on liquidity.** The opposite-side liquidity pool that the displacement was originally targeting.

Liquidity voids form on every market that the ICT methodology is applied to.

  * **GBP/USD** and **EUR/USD** — London-killzone displacement legs frequently leave voids on the way to the Asian-range sweep.

#### Does a Liquidity Void Get Filled Instantly?

There is no fixed time for the repricing or filling of a liquidity void.

Price may fill a liquidity void instantly, or it may return to it after a few days.

You cannot force price — but you can wait for it. Whenever price reprices the liquidity void, you can look for your trade setup at that level.

#### Will Price Fill the Liquidity Void For Sure?

No — it is not necessary for price to fill every liquidity void.

Price may create another liquidity void and continue its move without filling the previous one. Some voids stay unfilled for weeks.

#### Will Price Fill the Liquidity Void Completely?

Sometimes price may fill a liquidity void completely; sometimes it may not.

It is not necessary for price to fill a liquidity void completely. But you should wait for the [consequent encroachment](<https://innercircletrader.net/tutorials/ict-consequent-encroachment/> "Consequent Encroachment") (50% retracement) level of a liquidity void to be repriced before taking a trade. The 50% level is statistically the most reactive zone inside the void.

#### Liquidity Void vs Fair Value Gap

An [ICT Fair Value Gap](<https://innercircletrader.net/tutorials/fair-value-gap-trading-strategy/> "ICT Fair Value Gap") is a three-candle formation with a gap between the 1st and 3rd candlestick.

A liquidity void can be a formation of 3 or more candlesticks — and there can be 2 or more fair value gaps inside a single liquidity void.

In other words, the liquidity void is the broader imbalance container; the FVG is the discrete 3-candle unit. A void is “made of” FVGs, but the void itself is the displacement zone the algorithm tracks at a higher level.

#### Common Mistakes Around Liquidity Voids

These are the recurring mistakes I see when traders first start trading liquidity voids.

  1. **Trading every void.** Voids that form against the higher-timeframe bias are not respected. Only trade voids in the direction of the daily and H4 trend.
  2. **Expecting full fills.** Many voids fill only to the 50% (consequent encroachment) before reversing. Waiting for a full fill misses most of the move.
  3. **Confusing voids with FVGs.** A 3-candle FVG is a single imbalance unit. A void is a multi-candle displacement zone. Trading them with the same expectations leads to wrong stop placement.
  4. **No LTF MSS at the retest.** The retest of the void is not the entry. The lower-timeframe Market Structure Shift inside the void is the trigger.
  5. **Stop too tight.** Stops parked at the CE often get hunted on the second test. The opposite edge of the void is the conservative stop.
  6. **Forgetting age.** Older voids may not get filled at all, especially after price has structurally moved past them. Voids printed in the same week or the same session are highest priority.

#### FAQs about ICT Liquidity Void

Brief answers to the questions readers ask most often about the liquidity void.

**What is a liquidity void in trading?**

A liquidity void is the multi-candle imbalance left behind when price breaks a consolidation and moves strongly in one direction without internal retracement. It signals the absence of opposing orders in that price zone, and the algorithm typically returns to reprice the void on a later leg.

**What does liquidity void mean in forex?**

In forex (and on every market the ICT methodology is applied to), a liquidity void is the same concept — an imbalance zone created by a strong displacement move where no opposing liquidity slowed the move. The mechanic is identical to the indices and metals interpretation.

**How do I identify a liquidity void?**

Look for a strong directional break out of a consolidation, with multiple consecutive large-body candles whose wicks do not overlap the prior candle’s range. The price area covered by these displacement candles is the liquidity void.

**Will price always fill a liquidity void?**

No — price may create another void and continue without filling the previous one. Some voids stay unfilled for weeks. The probability of fill drops as price moves structurally further away.

**Where do I enter a liquidity void trade?**

After a higher-timeframe context confirms bias alignment and price retraces back into the void, wait for a lower-timeframe Market Structure Shift at the consequent encroachment (50%) of the void, then enter on the retest.

**Where do I place the stop loss?**

The conservative stop sits beyond the opposite edge of the liquidity void. Tighter stops can sit beyond the displacement candle’s high or low.

**Where do I take profit?**

The next draw-on-liquidity in the trend direction — typically the liquidity pool that the original displacement was targeting (the opposite side of the consolidation).

**What is the difference between liquidity void and fair value gap?**

A fair value gap is a single 3-candle gap. A liquidity void is a multi-candle zone that can contain two or more fair value gaps inside it. The void is the broader displacement container.

**Does the liquidity void work on indices and gold?**

Yes — NQ, ES and XAU/USD produce textbook liquidity voids around the New York AM session, the 09:50 macro and US economic releases.

**Does the liquidity void need to be filled before I can trade?**

No — you only need price to retrace back into the void to the consequent encroachment level before considering an entry. Full fills are not required.

**How long does it take to fill a liquidity void?**

There is no fixed time. Some voids fill within the same session; others stay unfilled for days or weeks. Recent voids (same week / same session) are the highest priority.
