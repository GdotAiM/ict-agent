#### What is the ICT 2022 Trading Strategy?

The ICT 2022 trading strategy is the algorithmic intraday model Michael Huddleston taught in his 2022 mentorship. It treats price as a delivery mechanism that institutions move through specific time windows in pursuit of [Liquidity](<https://innercircletrader.net/tutorials/liquidity-in-forex-trading/>) and the rebalancing of imbalances.

The strategy is built on three pillars: a higher-timeframe daily bias to anchor direction, a session liquidity sweep to mark the inflection, and a lower-timeframe entry trigger off a refined ICT PD Array. When all three line up, the trade is high probability. When even one is missing, I stand aside.

It is a complete intraday strategy that can yield 50 pips or more at a 1:3 risk-reward ratio when paired with a correct [ICT Daily Bias](<https://innercircletrader.net/tutorials/ict-daily-bias-trick/>).

**How the ICT 2022 Trading Strategy Works**

To trade the 2022 model successfully, I follow this sequence on every setup. The daily bias comes first — without it, the rest of the model is guesswork.

Once the bias is set, I wait for the London session to open at 03:00 AM New York local time and I let the model unfold from there.

#### Step-by-Step Trade Flow

This is the exact ten-step execution checklist I run before every 2022-model trade. Save it, print it, do not skip a step.

  1. **Establish daily bias** on the daily and 4-hour charts before the session opens.
  2. **Mark the price range** from NY-midnight (00:00 NY local) to the London open (03:00 NY local). The high and low of this range are your reference levels.
  3. **Wait for London open** at 03:00 AM NY local time. Do not pre-position.
  4. **Look for a liquidity sweep** of the previous range high or low in the direction opposite to your bias (a typical Judas move).
  5. **Watch for an ICT Market Structure Shift** on the lower timeframes (5-minute, 3-minute, or 1-minute) in the direction that aligns with your bias. See the dedicated [ICT Market Structure Shift](<https://innercircletrader.net/tutorials/ict-market-structure-shift/>) guide.
  6. **Confirm displacement** on the MSS leg. The candle that breaks the prior swing should close decisively, ideally leaving an imbalance behind. See [Displacement Move](<https://innercircletrader.net/tutorials/ict-displacement-move/>).
  7. **Mark the ICT PD Array** created during the displacement leg — fair value gap, order block, inverse fair value gap, or breaker block — using the [ICT PD Array](<https://innercircletrader.net/tutorials/ict-pd-array-key-to-trade-execution/>) framework.
  8. **Validate that the PD Array sits in the correct premium or discount zone** relative to the swept range, using the [premium and discount](<https://innercircletrader.net/tutorials/ict-premium-and-discount-zone-identification/>) framework.
  9. **Wait for price to retrace** back into the PD Array. Do not chase.
  10. **Execute the trade** at the PD Array tap, with stop loss beyond the swept extreme and take profit at the opposite end of the swept range or beyond.

The same ten-step flow applies at the New York open, with the New York range substituted for the London range when London has been quiet.

#### London-Session Trading Strategy

Before the London open, I always mark the high and low of the price range that has formed from NY-midnight (00:00 NY local) to London open (03:00 NY local). These two levels are the reference for every London setup.

When the London session opens, I look for price to take the high or low of that previous price range in line with my bias — that is the [Liquidity Sweep](<https://innercircletrader.net/tutorials/ict-liquidity-sweep-vs-liquidity-run/>).

After the liquidity sweep of the previous price range, I look for an [ICT Market Structure Shift](<https://innercircletrader.net/tutorials/ict-market-structure-shift/>) with a clean [Displacement Move](<https://innercircletrader.net/tutorials/ict-displacement-move/>) on the lower timeframes (5-minute or 3-minute, or even 1-minute when I have the experience to read it cleanly), on the opposite side of the sweep — aligning with the daily bias.

After the ICT market structure shift, I mark the [ICT PD Array](<https://innercircletrader.net/tutorials/ict-pd-array-key-to-trade-execution/>) — fair value gap, order block, inverse fair value gap, or breaker block — using the ICT premium and discount tool.

I then wait for price to trade back to the ICT PD Array sitting in the correct premium or discount zone.

When price tests the ICT PD Array, I execute the trade as shown in the example below.

#### Key Time-Frames for the ICT 2022 Trading Strategy

The 2022 model uses a clean timeframe ladder. I read each of these every session, in this order:

  * **Daily timeframe** — to set the ICT daily bias.
  * **1-hour timeframe** — for the higher-timeframe perspective and key levels.
  * **15-minute timeframe** — to identify the liquidity pools and price imbalances.
  * **5-minute, 3-minute, and 1-minute timeframes** — for the trade confirmation (MSS) and execution.

The 1-minute is the sharpest entry tool, but it is also the easiest to misread. I do not recommend it until you have at least a hundred logged 5-minute setups behind you.

#### Types of Liquidity for the ICT 2022 Trading Strategy

These are the six liquidity references I work with on every 2022-model session, in order of priority:

  1. Previous day high / low draw on liquidity.
  2. Previous session high / low draw on liquidity.
  3. Previous week high / low draw on liquidity.
  4. Established high / low on the 15-minute timeframe.
  5. Return to current or old week opening gap.
  6. Return to current or old day opening gap.

The first three carry the most weight because the orders sitting at those levels are the largest. The lower three are intraday refinements.

The New York session open is the highest-probability window for the 2022 model because it aligns with the ICT New York AM Session Kill Zone and overlaps with the tail of the London session. For US-based traders, this is also the cleanest window to trade because it lines up with morning hours in the New York and Eastern time zones — no late-night sessions required.

#### Common Mistakes I See Traders Make on the 2022 Model

Five mistakes show up in nearly every comment I receive on this strategy. Avoid these and the model converts at a much higher rate.

  1. **Trading without a daily bias.** The 2022 model is a directional strategy. Without a higher-timeframe bias, the liquidity sweep and the MSS are just noise. Set the bias on the daily before the session opens, then trade only in that direction.
  2. **Entering before the market structure shift confirms.** A liquidity sweep alone is not a signal. Price has to break the most recent counter-trend swing on the lower timeframe before I act. A wick poke into the PD Array without an MSS first is a setup that fails far more often than it works.
  3. **Stop loss too tight on the sweep.** The sweep candle often takes out a few pips beyond the prior extreme before reversing. If your stop sits one pip beyond the swing high or low, you are trading on the wrong side of the algorithm. Place stops beyond the wick of the sweep candle, with a small buffer.
  4. **Ignoring the kill-zone time window.** Setups that print outside the London Open or New York Open kill zones are generally lower quality. The 2022 model is built around specific times. If the chart pattern looks right but the clock says 11:30 AM NY, I usually skip.
  5. **Oversizing the position.** A 1:3 risk-reward target is realistic on this model, but only when stops are honoured. Risking 2% per trade is the upper bound I would suggest for anyone still in the first six months on this method. The discipline matters more than the entry technique.

#### FAQs About the ICT Trading Strategy

**What is the ICT 2022 Trading Strategy?**

The ICT 2022 Trading Strategy is the algorithmic intraday approach introduced by Michael Huddleston in his 2022 mentorship. It focuses on liquidity sweeps and price imbalances, with time and price as the two anchoring inputs that guide every trade.

**How does the ICT 2022 Trading Strategy work?**

The strategy revolves around identifying the correct daily bias, waiting for a session liquidity sweep at the London or New York open, confirming with a market structure shift on the lower timeframe, and executing on a refined PD Array entry in the right premium or discount zone.

**What is an ICT Daily Bias?**

ICT Daily Bias is the directional read of the market for the day — bullish or bearish — drawn from higher-timeframe price action and the next significant draw on liquidity. It is the first input in the 2022 model and the rest of the model relies on it being correct.

**What is a liquidity sweep in ICT trading?**

A liquidity sweep is when price takes out the stops sitting above a previous high or below a previous low, then reverses or accelerates. The sweep is the trigger event that opens the entry window for the 2022 model.

**What are the key timeframes for the ICT 2022 strategy?**

Daily timeframe for bias, 1-hour for higher-timeframe perspective, 15-minute for liquidity and imbalances, and 5-minute / 3-minute / 1-minute for confirmation and execution. The same ladder applies to indices and forex pairs alike.

**When should I trade using the ICT 2022 Trading Model?**

The two highest-probability windows are the London open at 03:00 AM New York time and the New York open at 08:00 AM New York time. Both overlap with kill zones and carry the volatility that the 2022 model needs.

**How is Fibonacci used in the ICT 2022 strategy?**

Fibonacci is used to mark Optimal Trade Entry (OTE) levels during retracements after major price moves, particularly after liquidity sweeps. The OTE zone identifies the discount or premium price levels for trade entries.

**What are the best currency pairs to trade using the ICT 2022 model?**

The model works well on US index futures (NASDAQ 100 / NQ and E-mini S&P 500 / ES) and on the major USD pairs — GBP/USD, EUR/USD, and XAU/USD. The cleaner the time-of-day delivery on the instrument, the better the model performs.

**Why does time matter so much in the ICT 2022 strategy?**

Price moves only when liquidity is available and volatility is concentrated. Both of these align with specific session windows — London open and New York open. Outside those windows, the model gives fewer high-probability setups.

**Can I trade the ICT 2022 model on a 1-minute chart?**

Yes, but the 1-minute timeframe demands experience. It magnifies wicks, false breaks, and noise. I recommend running at least a hundred logged 5-minute setups before moving down to the 1-minute for execution.

**What should I do if the London session is range-bound?**

If London remains range-bound, wait for the New York open. Mark the NY-midnight to NY-open range, then look for the New York session to sweep one side of the range and shift structure on the lower timeframe before entering.

**Is the ICT 2022 model profitable for beginners?**

The model is profitable when the rules are followed strictly. It is not easy, but it gives a clear bias and clear triggers. Beginners should expect six to twelve months of demo work before risking real capital. There is no shortcut.
