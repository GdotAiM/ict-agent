# How to Trade ICT Seek and Destroy Friday – ICT Trading Strategy

**Source:** [https://innercircletrader.net/tutorials/ict-seek-and-destroy-friday/](https://innercircletrader.net/tutorials/ict-seek-and-destroy-friday/)

![ICT Seek and Destroy Friday weekly profile chart showing Monday to Thursday consolidation and Friday two-sided liquidity sweep](https://innercircletrader.net/wp-content/uploads/2025/03/seek-and-destroy.png)

I lost three NFP Fridays in a row before I learned to spot a Seek and Destroy week. The market would chop sideways Monday to Thursday, I’d convince myself a setup was forming, take an early entry, and watch price knife through both my stop loss and the next obvious one before continuing in the direction I had originally bet on. Painful lessons.

**ICT Seek and Destroy Friday** is the weekly profile where price consolidates from Monday to Thursday in front of a high-impact economic event — usually NFP or FOMC — and then on Friday the market sweeps both sides of that consolidation range to clear liquidity from longs and shorts before the real move. It is a neutral to low-probability profile and the cardinal rule is simple: you do not trade before the news release.

After the news, price hunts the stops on one side, displaces hard, then continues in the direction of the weekly order flow toward whatever higher-timeframe draw on liquidity is sitting above or below. That post-news move is the trade.

In this guide, I’ll show you what the Seek and Destroy profile actually looks like on a chart, how to recognize a Seek and Destroy week before Friday arrives, how to handle it in bullish vs bearish markets, and the exact steps to take an entry once the news has done its work.

Don’t worry — you don’t need to predict the news. You just need to wait for the sweep, then enter on confirmation.

Ready? Let’s break it down.

## What is ICT Seek and Destroy Friday?

ICT Seek and Destroy Friday is a weekly profile where price consolidates Monday through Thursday and then aggressively sweeps both sides of that consolidation range on Friday in front of — or right at the moment of — a high-impact economic event.

It is part of the family of [ICT weekly profiles](<https://innercircletrader.net/tutorials/ict-weekly-range-profiles/> "ICT Weekly Range Profiles"). Most weeks have a directional bias from the open. This one doesn’t. On Seek and Destroy weeks, Smart Money is engineering liquidity above the range high (where stops from short sellers sit) and below the range low (where stops from buyers sit), so when the news drops, both pools can be cleared and the real move can continue without retail traders along for the ride.

In ICT terminology, Friday becomes a manipulation day. The accumulation has already happened during the Mon-Thu consolidation. The distribution comes after the sweep, in the direction of the higher-timeframe weekly order flow.

Many traders fall into the trap of overtrading during the Mon-Thu chop, only to get stopped out when Friday arrives and price runs both directions. Recognizing the profile early is half the battle.

## Why It Forms (The High-Impact News Setup)

Seek and Destroy weeks form when the market is waiting for an event big enough to reset positioning. The two main triggers:

  * **NFP (Non-Farm Payroll)** — first Friday of every month, 8:30 AM New York time. Moves USD pairs and indices.
  * **FOMC** — eight times a year, Wednesday 2:00 PM New York time. The Wednesday FOMC creates a similar sweep dynamic earlier in the week.

Other catalysts that can produce a Seek and Destroy week: CPI, central bank rate decisions, major political events. The common factor is **uncertainty large enough that institutional desks don’t want to position before the print** , so they let price coil while engineering liquidity at the extremes of the range.

This is the engineered-liquidity phase. The high of the Mon-Thu range becomes a buy-side liquidity pool. The low becomes a sell-side liquidity pool. Friday’s job is to take both before the actual directional move begins.

## How to Spot a Seek and Destroy Week

You can identify a Seek and Destroy week by Wednesday or Thursday — well before Friday’s news. Three confirming signs:

  1. **A high-impact event is on the calendar for Friday.** Check Forex Factory or your broker’s economic calendar for red-flagged events.
  2. **Monday-Thursday price action is sideways or shows irregular swings** — higher highs paired with lower lows, or low-range candles back and forth — instead of a clean directional drive.
  3. **Higher-timeframe weekly order flow has a clear bias** but the daily candles aren’t honoring it. That divergence (clear weekly direction + range-bound daily action) is the tell.

If all three are true, treat the rest of the week as Seek and Destroy. Do not take counter-trend entries. Do not chase mid-range fakeouts. Wait for Friday.

## ICT Seek and Destroy Friday in Bullish and Bearish Markets

The setup is symmetric. Same mechanic, opposite direction.

### (I) Bullish Market

In a typical bullish market, prices form higher highs and higher lows. During a Seek and Destroy week, that pattern stalls — the market either consolidates Mon-Thu or shows an irregular pattern of higher highs paired with lower lows as Smart Money positions for the news.

On Friday, after the news drops, price sweeps **sell-side liquidity** below the consolidation low first (clearing the stops of buyers) and then **displaces aggressively to the upside** in line with the weekly bullish bias.

How to trade it:

  1. **Avoid trading before the news release.** No exceptions.
  2. **Wait for the sell-side[liquidity sweep](<https://innercircletrader.net/tutorials/ict-liquidity-sweep-vs-liquidity-run/> "ICT Liquidity Sweep")** — price taking out the Mon-Thu range low after the news.
  3. **Watch for price to trade back into the range.** This is the institutional fingerprint.
  4. **Drop to a lower timeframe** (5m or 15m) and look for a [Market Structure Shift (MSS)](<https://innercircletrader.net/tutorials/ict-market-structure-shift/> "ICT Market Structure Shift") or a [Change in State of Delivery (CISD)](<https://innercircletrader.net/tutorials/ict-change-in-the-state-of-delivery/> "ICT CISD") to the upside.
  5. **Enter long** on the close of the MSS / CISD candle or a clean retest. Stop loss below the swept low.
  6. **Target the next higher-timeframe draw on liquidity** — the most recent significant high, the previous week’s high, or the daily / weekly bullish order flow target.

### (II) Bearish Market

In a typical bearish market, prices form lower highs and lower lows. During a Seek and Destroy week, that pattern stalls — the market either consolidates Mon-Thu or shows an irregular pattern of lower lows paired with higher highs.

On Friday, after the news drops, price sweeps **buy-side liquidity** above the consolidation high first (clearing the stops of sellers) and then **displaces aggressively to the downside** in line with the weekly bearish bias.

How to trade it:

  1. **Avoid trading before the news release.**
  2. **Wait for the buy-side liquidity sweep** — price taking out the Mon-Thu range high after the news.
  3. **Watch for price to trade back into the range.**
  4. **Drop to a lower timeframe** and look for an MSS or CISD to the downside.
  5. **Enter short** on the close of the MSS / CISD candle or a clean retest. Stop loss above the swept high.
  6. **Target the next higher-timeframe sell-side draw** — the most recent significant low, the previous week’s low, or the daily / weekly bearish order flow target.

## How to Trade Seek and Destroy Friday — Step by Step

The unified trading flow, regardless of bias direction:

  1. **Confirm the profile by Thursday.** High-impact news on Friday + range-bound Mon-Thu action + clear higher-timeframe weekly bias.
  2. **Mark the Mon-Thu range high and low.** These are your liquidity pools.
  3. **Stand aside until the news release.** Do not pre-position. Do not “scalp the chop”.
  4. **Watch which side gets swept first after the news.** The opposite side is your entry direction.
  5. **Wait for price to return to the range** — the sweep needs to be followed by a retracement back toward the middle.
  6. **Drop to a 5m or 15m chart** and look for an MSS or CISD in the direction opposite the sweep.
  7. **Enter on confirmation.** Stop just beyond the swept extreme. Target the next higher-timeframe draw on liquidity.

**Pro Tip** — The strongest Seek and Destroy trades happen when the post-sweep [displacement](<https://innercircletrader.net/tutorials/ict-displacement-move/> "ICT Displacement") leaves a clean Fair Value Gap or breaker block on the way back into the range. If price comes back to fill that imbalance before continuing, you get a second entry with even better risk-to-reward. Skip Seek and Destroy weeks where the higher-timeframe weekly order flow is unclear — without a known directional target, you have no reason to take the trade.

## Bonus Tip: Pairing With Weekly Profiles and Daily Bias

Seek and Destroy Friday is one of several [ICT weekly profiles](<https://innercircletrader.net/tutorials/ict-weekly-range-profiles/> "ICT Weekly Range Profiles"). The profile you’re in changes everything about which day you take the trade and which direction you expect the run.

Before you commit to a Seek and Destroy week, run through your higher-timeframe checklist:

  * **What is the weekly bias?** If unclear, sit out — no directional target means no trade.
  * **What is the[daily bias](<https://innercircletrader.net/tutorials/ict-daily-bias-explained/> "ICT Daily Bias") heading into Friday?** It should align with the post-sweep direction you expect.
  * **Which side is the obvious liquidity?** Smart Money will go for the largest pool first.

The Seek and Destroy entry is just the [Power of 3](<https://innercircletrader.net/tutorials/ict-power-of-3/> "ICT Power of 3") compressed into one Friday session — accumulation Mon-Thu, manipulation at the news, distribution after. Reading it through that frame makes the setup obvious.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the ICT Seek and Destroy Friday profile?

ICT Seek and Destroy Friday is a weekly profile where price consolidates from Monday through Thursday in front of a high-impact economic event (like NFP or FOMC), then on Friday sweeps both sides of the consolidation range to clear liquidity before continuing in the direction of weekly order flow.

### When does Seek and Destroy Friday usually happen?

Most often on the first Friday of the month (NFP) or in weeks containing FOMC, CPI, central bank rate decisions, or other red-flagged news events. Always check the economic calendar to identify candidate weeks.

### Should I trade before the news release on a Seek and Destroy Friday?

No. The cardinal rule of the profile is that you stand aside until the news has dropped and the liquidity sweep has happened. Pre-news entries get caught in the engineered moves on both sides of the range.

### How do I know which side will sweep first?

The side opposite to your expected post-news direction. If the higher-timeframe weekly order flow is bullish, expect the sell-side (low of the range) to be swept first. If bearish, expect the buy-side (high of the range) to be swept first.

### What confirmation do I use for entry after the sweep?

A Market Structure Shift or a Change in State of Delivery on a lower timeframe (5-minute or 15-minute), in the direction opposite the sweep. Do not enter on the sweep candle itself — wait for the structural confirmation that price has rejected the swept extreme.

### Is Seek and Destroy Friday the same as a stop hunt?

Stop hunting is the mechanism — the engineered liquidity grab. Seek and Destroy Friday is the weekly profile in which Smart Money does it twice (once on each side of the Mon-Thu range) before delivering the actual move. So a Seek and Destroy Friday contains stop hunts, but a stop hunt by itself doesn’t necessarily mean you’re in a Seek and Destroy week.

You wait for the sweep. Then — and only then — you take the entry.
