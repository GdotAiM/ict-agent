#### ICT Abbreviations List (A–Z)

**AMD** — Accumulation, Manipulation and Distribution. Also known as the [ICT Power of 3](<https://innercircletrader.net/tutorials/ict-power-of-3/>) daily template.

**BB** — [ICT Breaker Block](<https://innercircletrader.net/tutorials/ict-breaker-block-trading/>). The flipped order block that becomes opposite-direction support or resistance.

**BE** — Breakeven. Moving the stop loss to the entry price after a trade goes in your favor — if the stop is hit, no profit no loss.

**BISI** — Buy Side Imbalance Sell Side Inefficiency. The bullish [ICT Fair Value Gap](<https://innercircletrader.net/tutorials/fair-value-gap-trading-strategy/>).

**BOS** — [Break of Structure](<https://innercircletrader.net/tutorials/break-of-structure-bos/>). Price breaks the previous swing high or low after taking inducement, confirming continuation.

**BPR** — [Balanced Price Range](<https://innercircletrader.net/tutorials/ict-balanced-price-range-bpr/>). The overlap area between two opposite-direction fair value gaps in the same zone.

**BSL** — [Buy Side Liquidity](<https://innercircletrader.net/tutorials/liquidity-in-forex-trading/>). Buy stops at or above old highs, plus stop losses of sellers placed at or above the old highs.

**CBDR** — [Central Bank Dealers Range](<https://innercircletrader.net/tutorials/ict-central-bank-dealers-range/>). The 14:00–20:00 NY-time consolidation range used to project intraday targets.

**CE** — [Consequent Encroachment](<https://innercircletrader.net/tutorials/ict-consequent-encroachment/>). The 50% retracement level (midpoint) of an FVG, order block or any PD array.

**CHOCH** — [Change of Character](<https://innercircletrader.net/tutorials/change-of-character-choch-in-trading/>). The break of the most recent higher low (bullish trend) or lower high (bearish trend), confirming long-term trend reversal.

**CISD** — [Change in State of Delivery](<https://innercircletrader.net/tutorials/ict-change-in-the-state-of-delivery/>). The candle-level confirmation that price delivery has shifted direction.

**CRT** — [Candle Range Theory](<https://innercircletrader.net/tutorials/candle-range-theory-crt/>). The four-stage candle delivery model — open, sweep, reverse, close.

**D Bias / Daily Bias** — [ICT Daily Bias](<https://innercircletrader.net/tutorials/ict-daily-bias-trick/>). The anticipated direction of price for the day.

**ERL** — [External Range Liquidity](<https://innercircletrader.net/tutorials/ict-internal-external-liquidity/>). Liquidity residing outside a price range — the swing highs and lows.

**FTR** — Failed To Return. A smart-money zone where price failed to return after a structural break.

**FVG** — [ICT Fair Value Gap](<https://innercircletrader.net/tutorials/fair-value-gap-trading-strategy/>). A three-candle formation with a gap between the 1st and 3rd candlestick where price has not retraced previously.

**HRLR / LRLR** — [High-Resistance Liquidity Run / Low-Resistance Liquidity Run](<https://innercircletrader.net/tutorials/ict-hrlr-lrlr/>). The expected resistance level of a liquidity run before the algorithm reverses.

**HTF** — Higher Timeframe. 1-hour, 4-hour, 1-day, 1-week or 1-month.

**IDM** — [Inducement](<https://innercircletrader.net/tutorials/what-is-inducement-in-forex/>). The retail trap level smart money sweeps before delivering the real direction.

**iFVG** — [Inversion Fair Value Gap](<https://innercircletrader.net/tutorials/ict-inversion-fair-value-gap/>). A regular FVG that has been broken in the opposite direction and now acts as opposite-direction support or resistance.

**Implied FVG** — [Implied Fair Value Gap](<https://innercircletrader.net/tutorials/ict-implied-fair-value-gap-ifvg/>). A structural FVG implied by the wicks of three candles even when bodies overlap.

**IOFED** — [Institutional Order Flow Entry Drill](<https://innercircletrader.net/tutorials/ict-institutional-order-flow-entry-drill/>). The very edge of an FVG used as the earliest possible entry.

**IPDA** — [Inter-bank Price Delivery Algorithm](<https://innercircletrader.net/tutorials/ict-ipda/>). The algorithm that delivers price online on the charts.

**IRL** — Internal Range Liquidity. Liquidity residing inside a dealing range — the FVGs and order blocks.

**ITH** — [Intermediate Term High](<https://innercircletrader.net/tutorials/ict-advance-market-structure/>). A high in between two short-term highs.

**ITL** — [Intermediate Term Low](<https://innercircletrader.net/tutorials/ict-stl-itl-ltl/>). A low in between two short-term lows.

**LP** — [ICT Liquidity Pool](<https://innercircletrader.net/tutorials/ict-liquidity-pool/>). A cluster of stop-loss and pending orders at a key level.

**LTF** — Lower Timeframe. 1-minute, 5-minute or 15-minute.

**LTH** — Long Term High. A high with lower intermediate-term highs on both sides.

**LTL** — Long Term Low. A low with higher intermediate-term lows on both sides.

**MB** — [ICT Mitigation Block](<https://innercircletrader.net/tutorials/ict-mitigation-block-explained/>). The unmitigated swing point that price returns to before continuing.

**MMBM** — [Market Maker Buy Model](<https://innercircletrader.net/tutorials/ict-market-maker-buy-model/>). The institutional 4-phase bullish delivery template.

**MMSM** — [Market Maker Sell Model](<https://innercircletrader.net/tutorials/ict-market-maker-sell-model/>). The institutional 4-phase bearish delivery template.

**MSS** — [Market Structure Shift](<https://innercircletrader.net/tutorials/ict-market-structure-shift/>). The break of a swing high or low signaling the initial change in delivery direction.

**MT** — Mean Threshold. The 50% retracement level of an [ICT Order Block](<https://innercircletrader.net/tutorials/ict-order-block/>).

**NDOG** — [New Day Opening Gap](<https://innercircletrader.net/tutorials/ict-new-day-opening-gap-ndog/>). The daily gap between 5:00 PM close and 6:00 PM open in New York time.

**NWOG** — [New Week Opening Gap](<https://innercircletrader.net/tutorials/ict-new-week-opening-gap-nwog/>). The weekend gap between Friday close and Monday open.

**OB** — [ICT Order Block](<https://innercircletrader.net/tutorials/ict-order-block/>). The last opposing candle before the displacement that produced a structural break.

**OTE** — [Optimal Trade Entry](<https://innercircletrader.net/tutorials/ict-optimal-trade-entry-ote-pattern/>). The 62%–79% Fibonacci retracement zone of the most recent impulsive leg.

**PD Array** — Premium / Discount Array. The collective name for FVG, order block, breaker, mitigation, OTE and any other PD-arrangement zones used as entries.

**PDH** — Previous Day High. The high (or wick) of yesterday’s daily candle.

**PDL** — Previous Day Low. The low (or wick) of yesterday’s daily candle.

**PO3** — [ICT Power of Three](<https://innercircletrader.net/tutorials/ict-power-of-3/>). The Accumulation–Manipulation–Distribution daily template.

**QML** — [Quasimodo Pattern](<https://innercircletrader.net/tutorials/qml-chart-pattern/>). A reversal chart pattern at higher-timeframe support or resistance.

**RB** — Rejection Block. See [ICT Rejection Block](<https://innercircletrader.net/tutorials/ict-rejection-block/>).

**RDRB / RRPR** — [Redelivered / Rebalanced Price Range](<https://innercircletrader.net/tutorials/ict-redelivered-rebalanced-price-range/>). The PD-array reset where price returns to a prior FVG and rebalances it before continuing.

**SCOB** — [Single Candle Order Block](<https://innercircletrader.net/tutorials/single-candle-order-block-scob/>). The single-candle variant of the OB used for refined LTF entries.

**SIBI** — Sell Side Imbalance Buy Side Inefficiency. The bearish [ICT Fair Value Gap](<https://innercircletrader.net/tutorials/fair-value-gap-trading-strategy/>).

**SMT** — [Smart Money Technique Divergence](<https://innercircletrader.net/tutorials/ict-smt-divergence-smart-money-technique/>). The disagreement between two correlated pairs at a key liquidity level.

**SSL** — Sell Side Liquidity. Sell stops at or below old lows, plus stop losses of buyers placed at or below old lows.

**STH** — [Short Term High](<https://innercircletrader.net/tutorials/ict-swing-high-explained/>). A swing high — local extreme between two lower highs on either side.

**STL** — [Short Term Low](<https://innercircletrader.net/tutorials/ict-swing-low-formation/>). A swing low — local extreme between two higher lows on either side.

**TGIF** — [Thank God It Is Friday](<https://innercircletrader.net/tutorials/ict-tgif-setup-trade-like-a-pro/>). The Friday-only weekly-range retracement model.

#### Top 5 ICT Concepts You Must Know

If you are starting out, master these five abbreviations before any others — they appear in almost every ICT setup.

**(I) FVG** — [ICT Fair Value Gap](<https://innercircletrader.net/tutorials/fair-value-gap-trading-strategy/>). The three-candle imbalance that acts as the universal PD array.

**(II) MSS** — [ICT Market Structure Shift](<https://innercircletrader.net/tutorials/ict-market-structure-shift/>). The lower-timeframe break that triggers the entry.

**(III) PO3** — [ICT Power of 3](<https://innercircletrader.net/tutorials/ict-power-of-3/>). The daily Accumulation–Manipulation–Distribution template.

**(IV) OB** — [ICT Order Block](<https://innercircletrader.net/tutorials/ict-order-block/>). The institutional candle that price returns to before continuing.

**(V) OTE** — [Optimal Trade Entry](<https://innercircletrader.net/tutorials/ict-optimal-trade-entry-ote-pattern/>). The 62%–79% Fibonacci retracement entry zone.

#### FAQs about ICT Abbreviations

Brief answers to the questions readers ask most often about ICT abbreviations.

**What does ICT stand for in trading?**

ICT stands for Inner Circle Trader — the trading framework introduced by Michael J. Huddleston, built on the Interbank Price Delivery Algorithm and Smart Money Concepts.

**What is the difference between ICT and SMC?**

ICT and SMC overlap heavily — both are built on liquidity, imbalance and structure. SMC is essentially a derivative of ICT that some traders teach separately, but the mechanics are the same.

**What is the most important ICT abbreviation to learn first?**

FVG — the Fair Value Gap. Almost every ICT setup uses an FVG somewhere — as the entry, the target, or the bias confirmation.

**What does FVG stand for?**

FVG stands for Fair Value Gap — a three-candle formation with an unfilled gap between the 1st and 3rd candle wicks.

**What does BOS mean in trading?**

BOS stands for Break of Structure — price breaking the previous swing high or low after taking inducement, confirming the continuation of the trend.

**What does MSS stand for?**

MSS stands for Market Structure Shift — the break of a swing high or low signaling the initial change in price delivery direction.

**What does CHOCH mean?**

CHOCH stands for Change of Character — the break of the most recent structural higher low (bullish trend) or lower high (bearish trend), confirming long-term trend reversal.

**What does PO3 stand for?**

PO3 stands for Power of 3 — the daily Accumulation, Manipulation, Distribution template that describes how price is delivered each session.

**What does OTE stand for?**

OTE stands for Optimal Trade Entry — the 62%–79% Fibonacci retracement zone of the most recent impulsive leg, used as a refined entry zone.

**What does IPDA stand for?**

IPDA stands for Inter-bank Price Delivery Algorithm — the algorithm ICT teaches as the underlying engine that delivers price on the charts.

**What does IDM mean?**

IDM stands for Inducement — the retail-trap level smart money sweeps before delivering the real directional move.

**What does NWOG stand for?**

NWOG stands for New Week Opening Gap — the gap between Friday close and Monday open at the start of each week.

**What does NDOG stand for?**

NDOG stands for New Day Opening Gap — the daily gap between the 5:00 PM close and 6:00 PM open in New York time.

**What does CISD mean?**

CISD stands for Change in State of Delivery — the candle-level confirmation that price delivery has shifted direction.
