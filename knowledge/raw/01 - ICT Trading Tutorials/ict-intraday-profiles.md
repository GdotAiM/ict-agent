# ICT Intraday Profiles (Daily Profiles) — London Session Protraction Trading Model

**Source:** [https://innercircletrader.net/tutorials/ict-intraday-profiles/](https://innercircletrader.net/tutorials/ict-intraday-profiles/)

![ICT Intraday Profiles — daily-profile trading model based on the Central Bank Dealers Range, Asian range and London-session protraction](https://innercircletrader.net/wp-content/uploads/2024/10/ICT-Intraday-Profiles-2.png)

ICT Intraday Profiles — also called ICT Daily Profiles — are conceptual frameworks that describe typical patterns of price behaviour during a specific time of a trading day.

The intraday profiles depict the idea of a daily high during a sell day and a daily low during a buy day.

You can use ICT intraday profiles to find an intraday trade setup of 50 to 70 pips.

In this guide I walk you through the ICT intraday profiles — from their formation to their use in trading, with real market examples, the four sub-types (London Normal and London Delayed × Buy and Sell), the conditions checklist, the entry sequence, common mistakes and the FAQ.

#### What are ICT Intraday Profiles (Daily Profiles)?

ICT intraday profiles is a complete day-trading model based on the [ICT Central Bank Dealers Range (CBDR)](<https://innercircletrader.net/tutorials/ict-central-bank-dealers-range/>) and the [ICT Asian range](<https://innercircletrader.net/tutorials/ict-asian-range/>) liquidity sweep.

The ICT Central Bank Dealers Range forms between 02:00 PM and 08:00 PM New York local time.

The ICT Asian range lies between 08:00 PM and 12:00 AM New York local time.

For ICT intraday profiles, the CBDR should be less than 40 pips and the Asian range should be no more than 20 to 30 pips.

After 12:00 AM to 02:00 AM (NY local time), price should rally to create a [Judas Swing](<https://innercircletrader.net/tutorials/ict-judas-swing-complete-guide/>) above or below the CBDR (depending on the potential direction of the day) and then reverse.

#### Types of ICT Intraday Profiles

ICT intraday profiles have two major types depending on the directional bias of the day.

**(I)** ICT Intraday Sell Profile

**(II)** ICT Intraday Buy Profile

Each of the two splits into a London Normal Protraction sub-type and a London Delayed Protraction sub-type — making four total intraday profiles.

#### ICT Intraday Sell Profiles

ICT intraday sell profile is further divided into two types based on London-session protraction.

**(I) London Normal Protraction — Sell Profile**

To trade the ICT intraday sell profile with London-session normal protraction, check the following conditions.

(I) Bearish directional [bias](<https://innercircletrader.net/tutorials/ict-daily-bias-explained/>) for the day.

(II) Central Bank Dealers Range less than 40 pips.

(III) Asian range should not be more than 20 to 30 pips.

(IV) Price rallies up after 12:00 AM to 02:00 AM (NY local time).

(V) Protraction stage for the Judas Swing should not be more than 2 to 3 standard deviations of the CBDR.

If all the conditions for a London-session normal protraction are right, it is a valid intraday sell profile.

![ICT Intraday Sell Profile — London Normal Protraction with CBDR under 40 pips, Asian range 20-30 pips and Judas Swing rally between 12:00 AM and 02:00 AM](https://innercircletrader.net/wp-content/uploads/2024/10/ICT-intraday-profiles.png)

If you see one of these conditions in contrast then it is not a normal sell profile and you should let price do what it wants to do — but you can look for scalp trade setups.

**How to Trade the London Normal Protraction Sell Profile**

Once you have checked all the conditions for the London Normal Protraction Sell Profile, you are ready to take the sell trade.

You will see a price rally just after 12:00 AM, but a small little move down before the rally is also acceptable.

The trade entry may form as early as 01:00 AM or after 02:00 AM.

After the London-session protraction above the CBDR, wait for an [ICT Market Structure Shift](<https://innercircletrader.net/tutorials/ict-market-structure-shift/>) on a lower timeframe like 5-minute or lower.

After the MSS you can execute a sell trade in [premium](<https://innercircletrader.net/tutorials/ict-premium-and-discount-zone-identification/>) with stop loss above the London-session high.

For take profit you can target the higher-timeframe draw-on-liquidity or any discount [PD array](<https://innercircletrader.net/tutorials/ict-pd-array-key-to-trade-execution/>).

A real market example is shown below as depicted by ICT.

![ICT Intraday Sell Profile real market example — London Normal Protraction with rally above the CBDR, MSS to the downside and short entry from premium](https://innercircletrader.net/wp-content/uploads/2024/10/ict-intraday-sell-profile.png)

**(II) London Delayed Protraction Sell Profile**

This profile works when the market does not create the 12:00 AM price rally — and the market may or may not have a favourable CBDR.

Directional [bias](<https://innercircletrader.net/tutorials/ict-daily-bias-explained/>) is bearish but the London protraction is delayed (price does not rally after 12:00 AM).

The [Inter Bank Price Delivery Algorithm (IPDA)](<https://innercircletrader.net/tutorials/ict-ipda/>) will enter a protraction stage at or shortly after 02:00 AM.

Look for intraday premium PD arrays to short on retracement.

![ICT Intraday Sell Profile — London Delayed Protraction where the 12:00 AM rally is missing and IPDA protracts after 02:00 AM](https://innercircletrader.net/wp-content/uploads/2024/10/ICT-intraday-profiles-1.png)

**How to Trade the London Delayed Protraction Sell Profile**

To trade the London delayed protraction, you have to wait until the 02:00 AM price rally.

Once price rallies up after 02:00 AM, you mark the recent ICT dealing range and find the premium PD arrays.

When price reaches the premium PD array you can go short there, or you may wait for an ICT MSS on a lower timeframe for confirmation.

In this scenario your stop loss will be above the ICT dealing-range high, and for take profit you target the discount PD arrays or sell-side liquidity.

#### ICT Intraday Buy Profiles

ICT intraday buy profiles, based on the protraction behaviour during the London session, fall into two main types.

**(I) London Normal Protraction — Buy Profile**

For trading an ICT intraday buy profile with a normal London-session protraction, review the following conditions.

1\. **Bullish directional bias.** Confirm a bullish bias for the day.

2\. **CBDR (Central Bank Dealers Range).** Should be under 40 pips.

3\. **Asian range.** The range should ideally remain between 20 and 30 pips.

4\. **Price dip.** Expect a price dip between 12:00 AM and 02:00 AM (NY local time).

5\. **Judas Swing protraction.** Should not exceed 2 to 3 standard deviations of the CBDR.

If all conditions align, it is a valid intraday buy profile.

![ICT Intraday Buy Profile — London Normal Protraction with bullish bias, CBDR under 40 pips and Judas Swing dip between 12:00 AM and 02:00 AM](https://innercircletrader.net/wp-content/uploads/2024/10/ICT-intraday-buy-profile-nORMAL.png)

If any of these conditions diverge from this setup, it may not be an optimal buy profile. In such cases, allow price action to develop naturally, but you can still explore scalp-trading opportunities.

**How to Trade a London Normal Protraction Buy Profile**

Once you have confirmed the London Normal Protraction buy-profile conditions, look for the ideal buying opportunity.

After 12:00 AM, price should typically dip before a rally — though a slight move upward before the dip is acceptable.

Trade entries might present themselves as early as 01:00 AM or shortly after 02:00 AM.

After the London session price dips below the CBDR, wait for a Market Structure Shift (MSS) on a lower timeframe like the 5-minute chart.

Enter a buy trade in [discount](<https://innercircletrader.net/tutorials/ict-premium-and-discount-zone-identification/>), placing your stop loss below the London-session low.

For take profit, target higher-timeframe liquidity pools or premium PD arrays.

An example of an ICT intraday buy profile (London Normal Protraction) is shown below.

![ICT Intraday Buy Profile real market example — London Normal Protraction with dip below the CBDR, MSS to the upside and long entry from discount](https://innercircletrader.net/wp-content/uploads/2024/10/buy-profile-normal.png)

**(II) London Delayed Protraction — Buy Profile**

This buy profile applies when price does not dip as expected after 12:00 AM. Here, the London protraction is delayed, meaning price does not drop immediately after 12:00 AM, though the overall directional bias remains bullish.

In this scenario, the IPDA (Interbank Price Delivery Algorithm) will enter a protraction phase at or shortly after 02:00 AM. Focus on intraday discount PD arrays for entry on retracements.

![ICT Intraday Buy Profile — London Delayed Protraction where the 12:00 AM dip is missing and IPDA protracts after 02:00 AM](https://innercircletrader.net/wp-content/uploads/2024/10/ICT-intradayt-buy-profile-london-delayed-1.png)

**How to Trade a London Delayed Protraction Buy Profile**

To trade a delayed protraction setup, watch for a price dip around 02:00 AM.

Once price dips after 02:00 AM, mark the recent ICT dealing range and locate the discount PD arrays.

As price nears the discount PD array, you may either take a long position or wait for an ICT MSS on a lower timeframe to confirm your entry.

In this case, set your stop loss below the ICT dealing-range low — and for take profit, target the premium PD arrays or buy-side liquidity.

#### Step-by-Step ICT Intraday Profile Trade Flow

This is the exact sequence I run before placing a trade off the daily profile.

  1. **Set the daily directional bias.** Bullish or bearish on the daily and H4 — neutral days do not produce a clean profile.
  2. **Mark the CBDR.** 02:00 PM to 08:00 PM NY local. Confirm the range is under 40 pips.
  3. **Mark the Asian range.** 08:00 PM to 12:00 AM NY local. Confirm the range is 20 to 30 pips.
  4. **Plot the standard-deviation projections.** 1, 2 and 3 standard deviations of the CBDR above and below — these are the Judas Swing limits.
  5. **Watch the 12:00 AM to 02:00 AM window.** Bearish day — expect a rally above CBDR. Bullish day — expect a dip below CBDR.
  6. **Classify the profile.** If the move arrives between 12:00 and 02:00 it is a London Normal Protraction. If it arrives only after 02:00 it is a London Delayed Protraction.
  7. **Wait for the lower-timeframe MSS.** 5-minute or lower for the entry trigger.
  8. **Enter on the retest.** In premium for shorts, in discount for longs.
  9. **Set the stop.** Above the London-session high (shorts) or below the London-session low (longs).
  10. **Take profit at the next draw on liquidity.** Higher-timeframe DOL, opposite-side PD array or 50 to 70 pips by default.

#### Best Time Frame for ICT Intraday Profiles

You use the 1-day timeframe as the parent chart to find the directional bias of the day.

For protraction observation and trade execution, use lower timeframes — 15-minute or preferably lower (5-minute, 1-minute).

The 5-minute chart is the timeframe I personally use for the MSS trigger; the 1-minute is used to time the actual fill.

#### Best Pair for ICT Intraday Profiles

Initially these concepts were introduced and tested on the index market — the NQ futures and E-mini — by Michael Huddleston.

Over time traders began applying these for forex and metals, and they delivered extraordinary results in those markets as well.

You can use them in any market, but it is recommended to back-test these concepts on a demo account before using them on a live account.

**For traders in the United States** who follow the CFTC FIFO and no-hedge rules, the intraday profile model is most natural on NQ and ES futures (CME Group). The 12:00 AM to 02:00 AM Judas Swing window aligns with the late-Asian / early-London handover, and the MSS entry typically prints inside the late-London or early-NY-AM session — fully accessible to US-based traders before working hours.

#### Best ICT Intraday Profile to Trade

The London Normal Protraction is the best intraday profile both for bullish and bearish days.

In London Delayed Protraction, sometimes price may rally after 02:00 AM and then not reverse — so it can be tricky.

If you are new to the framework, focus on London Normal Protraction trades for the first 30 days of demo before adding the Delayed variant.

#### Common Mistakes Around ICT Intraday Profiles

These are the recurring mistakes I see when traders first start trading the daily profile model.

  1. **Skipping the CBDR / Asian-range size check.** CBDR over 40 pips or Asian range over 30 pips invalidates the profile. Trading anyway produces unreliable results.
  2. **Pre-positioning before the Judas Swing.** The protraction is the catalyst. Entering before the 12:00 AM to 02:00 AM move locks you into the wrong side of the swing.
  3. **Mis-classifying Normal vs Delayed.** If the move only comes after 02:00 AM, that is a Delayed setup, not a missed Normal. Treat it differently — entries in delayed profiles use the dealing-range high/low instead of the London-session high/low for the stop.
  4. **No MSS confirmation.** The 5-minute MSS is the trigger. Entering on the protraction wick alone front-runs the setup.
  5. **Stop too tight.** Stops parked exactly at the London-session high or low get hunted on the second test. Leave a small buffer.
  6. **Ignoring the bias.** A bearish CBDR sweep on a bullish day is a continuation, not a reversal. Always reference the daily directional bias first.

#### FAQs about ICT Intraday Profiles

Brief answers to the questions readers ask most often about the intraday / daily profile model.

**What are ICT intraday profiles?**

ICT intraday profiles are a day-trading framework that maps how the Central Bank Dealers Range, the Asian range and the 12:00 AM to 02:00 AM Judas Swing combine to set up a directional move during the London or NY-AM session.

**What is the difference between ICT intraday profile and ICT daily profile?**

They are the same concept. “Intraday profile” emphasises the within-day execution; “daily profile” emphasises the daily-bias context. Both refer to the same model.

**What are the four ICT intraday profiles?**

(1) London Normal Protraction Sell, (2) London Delayed Protraction Sell, (3) London Normal Protraction Buy, (4) London Delayed Protraction Buy.

**What CBDR size makes a profile valid?**

The CBDR must be less than 40 pips. A wider CBDR invalidates the profile because the standard-deviation projections become too wide to be reliable Judas Swing limits.

**What Asian-range size makes a profile valid?**

The Asian range should be 20 to 30 pips — tight enough to indicate the consolidation that precedes the protraction.

**When should I enter the intraday profile trade?**

After the 12:00 AM to 02:00 AM Judas Swing protracts above (sell day) or below (buy day) the CBDR, wait for a 5-minute or lower MSS, then enter on the retest in premium (sell) or discount (buy).

**Where do I place the stop loss?**

Above the London-session high for sell trades and below the London-session low for buy trades. For Delayed Protraction, use the ICT dealing-range high/low instead.

**What is the take profit on an intraday profile trade?**

The higher-timeframe draw-on-liquidity, the opposite-side PD array, or a 50 to 70 pip default target.

**Which is the best intraday profile to trade?**

London Normal Protraction — both for sell and buy days. The Delayed variant is workable but requires patience after 02:00 AM and is more prone to one-way runs that fail to reverse.

**Does the intraday profile work on indices and gold?**

Yes — NQ and ES were the original tested instruments, and gold (XAU/USD) and major forex pairs (GBP/USD, EUR/USD) deliver clean profiles when the CBDR and Asian-range conditions are met.

**What is the standard deviation of CBDR?**

The standard deviation is the multiple of the CBDR size projected above and below the range. ICT uses 2 and 3 standard deviations as the Judas Swing limits — beyond those, the protraction is too wide and the profile loses reliability.
