# ICT Tradings Editorial Process

**Source:** [https://innercircletrader.net/editorial-process/](https://innercircletrader.net/editorial-process/)

ICT Tradings was founded in 2023 by Ayub Rana & Waris Salman Shah as a go to ICT Trading resource site for newbies to pro traders.

Over the last 1 year, ICT Tradings has become the world’s largest free ICT resource site with 100+ tutorials covering ICT strategies specifically & forex trading in general.

#### Our Editorial Team and Editorial Process

Our editorial team at ICT Tradings is a group of ICT & Forex experts led by the qualified financial expert Ayub Rana. 

Instead of freelance content writers, we have full time real ICT experts who have 5+ years of hand on experience in ict trading with a proven track record.

At ICT Tradings, our experienced editorial team meticulously oversees every piece of content we publish. Each article undergoes several phases of our editorial process, with multiple team members involved in each stage. 

This thorough review ensures that all content is rigorously fact-checked and verified for accuracy and integrity before publication.

#### How We Write ICT Trading Tutorials

As the forex is a very sensitive field so we take it very seriously before publishing any piece of content. Each ICT Trading tutorial is written after extensive market research & back testing the strategy itself by our real ICT experts on real trading accounts.

Every possible effort is made to fully mention the pros & cons of any trading strategy but due to the nature of the financial markets, no can be foolproof, so we strictly advise to consider risk management before practicing any ICT strategy.

We only recommend strategies & products that we believe will add value to our readers.

All opinions are our own, and we do not accept payments for positive reviews of a bad strategy or product.

#### We Fact Check for Accuracy and Integrity

Each piece of content at ICT Tradings is carefully reviewed by our editors to ensure it is comprehensive, factually accurate, and in line with our strict editorial standards. Following this review, the Senior Content Manager performs a final check for accuracy and integrity.

Additionally, our Review Board collaborates closely with the editorial team, providing essential insights to ensure our content maintains high quality. The board’s recommendations and feedback are communicated directly to the editorial team for continuous improvement.

#### Up-to-Date Content 

At ICT Tradings, we make every effort to keep the content up to date & according to financial markets trends. We routinely review our existing content every few months to ensure all information, including trading tutorials & products remains relevant.

#### How is ICT Tradings Funded?

Running a popular website like ICT Tradings, which assists thousands of users every month, requires a substantial investment in both server infrastructure and content creation.

Our team produces and maintains hundreds of free ICT tutorials.

To sustain our operations and continue providing valuable content, we need to generate revenue, otherwise we would have to shut the site down.

Below are the two primary sources of funding for ICT Tradings.

#### Ads

We generate revenue by displaying ads from advertisers, such as Google AdSense, on our website. These ads help us fund the necessary infrastructure and content creation efforts. 

The revenue from these advertisements allows us to continue providing free ICT tutorials to our users.

#### Referral Fees

We earn referral fees when you sign up with the trading companies & products we recommend.

We only endorse products that we genuinely believe will benefit our readers. Each product we recommend undergoes a deep testing and evaluation. 

All opinions expressed are our own, and we do not accept payments in exchange for positive reviews of a bad product.
