## What Is Inner Circle Trader (ICT) Trading?

**Inner Circle Trader (ICT)** is a price-action trading methodology developed by Michael J. Huddleston. It teaches traders to read the market the way institutions do — by tracking **liquidity**, **order flow**, and the algorithmic time windows that drive most intraday moves. The "ICT" in this context refers to the trading framework, not Information & Communications Technology.

The method is built on six interlocking concepts:

1. **PD Array (Premium / Discount Array)** — the structural map of a trading range
2. **ICT Kill Zones** — Asian, London, New York AM & PM
3. **Order Blocks** — institutional accumulation / distribution
4. **Fair Value Gaps (FVG)** — imbalances algorithms revisit
5. **Liquidity** — the fuel every move runs on
6. **Power of 3** — accumulation / manipulation / distribution

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is ICT trading?
ICT trading is the smart-money trading methodology developed by Michael Huddleston. Inner Circle Trader teaches that price is delivered by institutions through repeatable patterns called PD Arrays — order blocks, fair value gaps, liquidity pools, and kill-zone time windows.

### Who is the Inner Circle Trader?
The Inner Circle Trader is Michael Huddleston. He shares the method through long-form mentorship videos on YouTube. The site innercircletrader.net, run by Ayub Rana, organises and teaches these concepts with annotated charts.

### What should beginners learn first?
Start with: PD Array, Order Block, Fair Value Gap, and Kill Zones. Then add Liquidity, Market Structure (BOS, CHoCH, MSS), and Power of 3 (AMD).

### Is ICT free?
The ICT method itself is free on YouTube. Tutorials on innercircletrader.net are free to read. An optional consolidated PDF is sold separately.
