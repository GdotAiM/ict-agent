## What Is Inner Circle Trader (ICT) Trading?

**Inner Circle Trader (ICT)** is a price-action trading methodology developed by [Michael J. Huddleston](<https://innercircletrader.net/opinion/who-is-inner-circle-trader-ict/>). It teaches traders to read the market the way institutions do — by tracking **liquidity** , **order flow** , and the algorithmic time windows that drive most intraday moves. The "ICT" in this context refers to the trading framework, not Information & Communications Technology.

The method is built on six interlocking concepts. Once these are second nature, almost any ICT setup decomposes into them:

  * [PD Array (Premium / Discount Array)](<https://innercircletrader.net/tutorials/ict-pd-array-key-to-trade-execution/>) — the structural map of a trading range
  * [ICT Kill Zones](<https://innercircletrader.net/tutorials/master-ict-kill-zones/>) — Asian, London, New York AM & PM
  * [Order Blocks](<https://innercircletrader.net/tutorials/ict-order-block/>) — institutional accumulation / distribution
  * [Fair Value Gaps (FVG)](<https://innercircletrader.net/tutorials/fair-value-gap-trading-strategy/>) — imbalances algorithms revisit
  * [Liquidity](<https://innercircletrader.net/tutorials/liquidity-in-forex-trading/>) — the fuel every move runs on
  * [Power of 3](<https://innercircletrader.net/tutorials/ict-power-of-3/>) — accumulation / manipulation / distribution

Every tutorial on this site is written by a working ICT trader, from real charts and real trades — no theory-only filler. The full curriculum is free, organised from foundations through advanced setups, and updated for 2025–2026.

## Frequently Asked Questions About ICT Trading

What is ICT trading?

ICT trading is the smart-money trading methodology developed by Michael Huddleston, an American trader and educator. Inner Circle Trader teaches that price is delivered by institutions through repeatable patterns called PD Arrays — order blocks, fair value gaps, liquidity pools, and kill-zone time windows. ICT here means the trading method, not the Information & Communications Technology abbreviation.

Who is the Inner Circle Trader?

The Inner Circle Trader is Michael Huddleston, the American trader who developed the ICT methodology over more than two decades of work in the futures and forex markets. He shares the method through long-form mentorship videos on his YouTube channel. This site, run by Ayub Rana, organises and teaches Huddleston's concepts in plain English with annotated charts.

What are the most important ICT concepts to learn first?

Start with the four foundations: PD Array, Order Block, Fair Value Gap, and Kill Zones. Once those are clear, add Liquidity, Market Structure (BOS, CHoCH, MSS), and Power of 3 (AMD). The free ICT PDF on this site sequences these concepts in the same order I learned them and the same order I would teach again.

Is ICT trading profitable for beginners?

ICT trading is profitable when the rules are followed strictly and trading discipline is in place. The method is not easy, but it gives you a clear bias and clear entry triggers. Beginners should expect six to twelve months of demo work before risking real capital. There is no shortcut, and any source promising one is misleading you.

Is the ICT method free or paid?

The ICT method itself is free — Michael Huddleston has released the complete mentorship on YouTube at no cost. Every tutorial on innercircletrader.net is also free to read. The only paid product on this site is the optional 160-page consolidated PDF, which is a convenience for traders who prefer one structured download over scrolling 110 articles.
