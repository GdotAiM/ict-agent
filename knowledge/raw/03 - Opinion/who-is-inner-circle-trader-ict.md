#### Introduction to Inner Circle Trader (ICT)

Michael J Huddleston, also known as Inner Circle Trader, is a US-based trader primarily focused on indices trading such as NASDAQ, S&P 500 and Dow Jones.

He gained popularity through his YouTube channel named Inner Circle Trader, where he shares his private teachings for free.

He is popular around the globe because of his unique concepts and his accuracy.

He first talked about algorithmic trading when most retail traders were using chart patterns like the butterfly pattern and flag pattern.

He claimed that the market does not move because of any pattern or simple buying and selling pressure.

He introduced the concept of algorithm and liquidity, which states that the market does not move randomly — there is something controlling price delivery, and there are people behind it whom he calls Smart Money.

#### Meaning Behind the Inner Circle Trader Logo

The Inner Circle Trader (ICT) logo, used by Michael J. Huddleston, is a personal brand logo that represents his trading philosophy and methodology.
